## Notable Events

- *The Wyndham Scheme* (1967): Introduction of an additional year of high school in New South Wales, raising the standard age of completion to 18 and broadening the curriculum.
- *Leaving Certificate Examinations*: The final set of public exams in New South Wales until 1966, marking the end of high school for many students.

## Commentary

The evolution of mathematics and science education has been shaped by curriculum changes, examination standards, and broader social developments. Historical exam papers and curricula provide insight into the expectations and challenges faced by students and educators in different eras.
